## Orange Cyberdefense Datalake

Powered by Orange Cyberdefense Datalake platform, this threat intelligence integration provides insights on IP addresses, domains and various other indicators for threat investigations.

### Capabilities

- Enrich your detections with knowledge from kinds of technical indicators like IP, URL, Domain and Files.
- Develop playbooks to automate enrichment and qualification of incidents.
- Alternatively, setup this integration as a feed to continuously fetch and import threat intelligence.
